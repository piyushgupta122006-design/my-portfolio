BNN CS Study Hub (FYCS Library Hub)

The centralized digital academic portal for BNN College CS students. Streamlines access to first-year subject notes, previous question papers, assignments, and real-time notifications.

React.js Firebase Auth Firestore ES6

My Role: Full-stack developer — engineered the React frontend, Firebase Auth/Firestore database, and admin management panel.

Live Impact: Adopted by 107+ registered student users with 117+ study documents across 29 subjects and 1.2k+ page views.
